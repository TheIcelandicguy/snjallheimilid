Services

Lights and switching

One module per lighting group in the ceiling box. Your switches keep working, but the lights also respond to schedules, motion sensors and dimming.

Sockets and energy

Products on offer

This is the material the estimate counts. What suits your house becomes clear once your answers are in.

One Shelly module per light group, in the ceiling box or in the board on a DIN rail. The lights switch on and off from the wall switch as before, and also from the app, on a timer or from a motion sensor.

A dimmer for each dimmed light group, in the ceiling box, or in the board where two groups share one module. An ordinary rocker switch only switches on and off, so dimmed groups get a spring-return push button: hold it to dim.

Existing switches and sockets are kept where that suits. New switches and sockets are quality designer fittings with rocker and frame. A wireless button or a Shelly i4 in the switch box switches without an extra wire.

One module per motorised window. The curtains move by time or scene and can also be controlled in the app.

One module per heating zone, radiators or floor heating, and a thermostat in each zone: a Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensor, or a Shelly wall display, which is both a switch and a thermostat.

An energy meter in the board shows the house's consumption. The board can also hold Shelly Pro modules on a DIN rail and smart circuit breakers that can be switched off remotely and show the load. One breaker is counted per circuit.

Motion, temperature and humidity sensors in the rooms and sensors on the outer doors. They are wireless and talk Bluetooth to the nearest Shelly module or Bluetooth gateway.

COB strips in RGB, RGB with tunable white or tunable white (CCT), with a driver and a controller that handles the colours. The strips are drawn as linear lights on the plan and counted per metre.

Home Assistant, either as a small hub or a small computer, if you want everything in one place. Without a hub the modules work in the Shelly app.

The systems we work with

Three systems, chosen to suit the house and what you want. Shelly is the default in the estimate; Plejd and Tuya are options.

Shelly

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth modules that go in the box or the board. They work without the cloud, talk to Home Assistant and cover lights, dimming, curtains, heating and energy metering.

Plejd

A Swedish Bluetooth-mesh system for lighting. The modules talk to each other and strengthen the network, it works without an internet connection and is set up in the Plejd app. A gateway is needed to control it from away. There is no official Home Assistant support, only community solutions.

Tuya (Zigbee and Wi-Fi)

An open platform with many manufacturers, for example BSEED. Suits cases where price matters and the look of designer switches is important: glass switches, sockets with USB and thermostats. Works with Zigbee and Home Assistant. Certification, quality and warranty must be checked for each product.

Wiring accessories

Switches, sockets and frames, chosen for looks and price. A frame is sold separately and depends on how many units sit in the box, from one to five; BSEED also sells its frame separately.

Legrand Mosaic N

A white system that clips onto a mounting frame. Frames for one to four units in a row; from two units a mounting frame goes under the frame.

Berker S1

A German design system where the mechanism, the rocker and the frame are separate parts. Frames from one to five.

Gira S55

A design system with a rocker and a frame, laid out like Berker: mechanism, rocker and frame, frames from one to five.

BSEED

Zigbee switches and sockets in glass or plastic on the Tuya platform, the mechanism with the switch built in. The frame is sold separately by the number of units, from one to five.

About Snjallheimilið

Snjallheimilið is Davíð in Akureyri. Years of experience in electrical work, training in computer and network systems, and a home of his own that has been smart for years, running software he writes himself and publishes as open source for Home Assistant.

The solutions are built on Shelly modules: they sit in the existing boxes, work on the home network without a cloud, and talk to Home Assistant, Google and Apple. No subscription, and no lock-in to one brand beyond what you choose yourself.

Connection to the house's electrical installation is done by a licensed electrician in accordance with the Icelandic electrical regulations. Snjallheimilið takes care of the design, the materials and the software setup.
