Services

Lights and switching

One module per lighting group in the ceiling box. Your switches keep working, but the lights also respond to schedules, motion sensors and dimming.

Sockets and energy

An energy meter in the distribution board shows what the house uses and when. Metered sockets switch off what was left on.

Heating and curtains

Heating by zone, curtains that follow the sun. All controlled from one app or Home Assistant.

Drawing and parts list

A wiring drawing with IEC symbols, cable runs, wires and the distribution board. A parts list with a price range from Icelandic shops, so your electrician can get straight to work.

The estimate covers materials and design only: labour, boxes, conduit and travel come into the quote once the house has been looked at.

  1. You answer questions. Rooms, lights, switching, switches and sockets, what to dim, which equipment you want and whether the wiring is new. Two minutes on your phone.

About Snjallheimilið

Snjallheimilið is Davíð in Akureyri. Years of experience in electrical work, training in computer and network systems, and a home of his own that has been smart for years, running software he writes himself and publishes as open source for Home Assistant.

The solutions are built on Shelly modules: they sit in the existing boxes, work on the home network without a cloud, and talk to Home Assistant, Google and Apple. No subscription, and no lock-in to one brand beyond what you choose yourself.

Connection to the house's electrical installation is done by a licensed electrician in accordance with the Icelandic electrical regulations. Snjallheimilið takes care of the design, the materials and the software setup.
