# Snjallheimilið (heimasíða)

Public home page of Snjallheimilið (Davíð, Akureyri): one static page, Icelandic, phone first, GitHub Pages.
Source is this repo on `E:\snjallheimilid` (repo `TheIcelandicguy/snjallheimilid`, public). Nothing goes to `Z:`;
deploy = push to `main`; Pages serves the root at https://snjallheimilid.is (the `CNAME` file; owner 2026-11-26: the spelling with d is the
address, the same as the mail's, and the one with ð, registered first on 2026-11-02 (xn--snjallheimili-gkb.is), forwards to it. DNS at
Cloudflare: A/AAAA records of GitHub Pages on the apex, CNAME `www` and `aetlun` to theicelandicguy.github.io, none of them proxied).
The estimate page lives in the private `smart-home-estimator` repo, served as https://aetlun.snjallheimilid.is.

- `site.css` — same colour tokens as quick.css in smart-home-estimator (light and dark by `prefers-color-scheme`),
  880 px wrap, cards, numbered steps, form.
- `#home-assistant` — a card above the three systems in `#kerfi` (owner 2026-11-24): what Home Assistant is and does, in plain terms, four points. The text is a draft for the owner; a screenshot of his own Home Assistant is to be added when he sends one (the repository is public: nothing private in it).
- `tools/lines_view.py` — the drawn view of the fitting lines on a wall (owner 2026-11-24: the lines shown "in a more illustrated view", on walls of different colours, with everything each offers). It writes the block between the `lines-view:start` and `lines-view:end` comments in `#innlagnaefni`: a set per line (`.lv-set`), a frame per kind of part plus a few frames of several positions, each an inline SVG of our own drawing (flat shapes, no maker's picture) with its name under it. What each line offers is the list LINES in the script, copied by hand from the estimate's tables (smart-home-estimator: FIT_LINES in prices.js and quickKindsFor in quick.js) that day: a part added there is added here, then this script and `tools/i18n.py` are run. site.css paints the drawings from variables (`data-fit` on `.lv` = white, black, grey or gold; `data-line` = the corner radius and the gloss of glass; `--wall` = the wall), and the `.lv` block of site.js shows one line at a time, paints the wall from six swatches, offers the four colours on BSEED's two lines only. Owner 2026-11-25: the drawings do nothing when pressed (the pressing and the now-and-then animation of the first version are out). Without script every set shows. The script also writes missing en / es / pl texts into `i18n/` (es / pl are Claude's). Mosaic also shows its frames counted in units (owner 2026-11-25: three of them, 3, 4 and 6 units, one long opening filled with narrow and wide modules) and its plate with rounder lower corners. What each line offers, colour by colour, is `tools/lines.json` (owner 2026-11-25: one source): it is written by the estimator's tool lines_offer.mjs from the table the estimate books from (`node tools/lines_offer.mjs E:/snjallheimilid/tools/lines.json` in that repository) and holds the kinds of part only, no item numbers and no prices. Kept by hand in the script: the tabs, which parts are the smart ones and the example frames of several positions. A part that is not made in every colour of its line carries `data-fits`, and site.js shows it with those colours only (BSEED: no doorbell in gold glass, no thermostats in grey or gold plastic, no HDMI in grey plastic).
- `tools/lines3d/` — the same lines in 3D (owner 2026-11-24 and 25: closer to the real product, more detail, stills for now). `tools/lines3d/lines3d.py` runs in Blender without a window and builds everything from boxes and cylinders, each line to its own measurements (frame size, thickness, opening, centre part and how far it stands proud; from Legrand's and Schneider's sheets and BSEED's product pictures where they state them, marked est. in the script where not). They are look-alikes, not the makers' models, and carry no logo. With `--batch` it renders every frame of every line and colour alone on a see-through wall that keeps only the shadow (242 pictures since the lists follow the colours, about half an hour on the RTX 3060; `--parts heatw,heate` renders chosen boxes only); `tools/lines3d/to_webp.py` trims them and writes `img/lines3d/<line>-<colour>-<part>.webp`. In the view the choice Teikning / Þrívídd (`.lv-mode`, `data-mode` on `.lv`) swaps each drawing for its picture (`.lv-pic`, laid by `--k` so the frame sits where the drawing sat); site.js sets a picture's address only when the 3D view is chosen, so the pictures are not downloaded otherwise. What is rendered (the script's PARTS) is read from tools/lines_view.py, so the two cannot differ. The two thermostats are told apart by a mark on the screen, waves for water and a bolt for electric, as in the drawn view. A close-up of each line with a room's light (`--hero`) is `img/lines3d/hero-<line>.webp`, shown over the line's pictures in the 3D view (`.lv-hero`) and fetched only then.
- `vorur.html` — the product page (owner 2026-11-27: a full product view with a filter like a shop's), written whole by `tools/products.py`, never by hand: a card per part of each fitting line (77), with its computer-made picture from `img/lines3d/`, the line's name and a dot per colour it is made in; filters by line, colour, kind (the script's CATS) and smart or plain, each choice with a count. What is on it is `tools/lines.json` through tools/lines_view.py, so it shows what the estimate books and nothing else: no prices, no item numbers. `vorur.js` filters: ticks of one group are alternatives, the groups narrow each other, the count beside a choice is what it would show with the other groups as they stand (a choice that shows nothing is greyed), a colour picked shows the pictures in that colour, and the choices are kept in the address (`?line=…&fit=…&cat=…&smart=…`) so a filtered list can be sent as a link. Under 800 px the filters open from the button `#pvToggle`; without script every card shows and the filters are hidden. index.html links to it under the lines view. Run `python tools/products.py` (from `tools/`, it imports lines_view) and then `tools/i18n.py`, which since then writes every page of its PAGES list into en/, es/ and pl/ and keeps a link from one page to another inside the language's folder. The frame builder on the same page (owner 2026-11-27, `#pb`): the plus on a card, or a drag of the card, puts the part into a frame that takes the line and the colour of its first part; only parts of that line made in that colour go in, the frame grows to the largest the line has in the colour (`tools/frames.json`, written like lines.json by the estimator's lines_offer.mjs: `node tools/lines_offer.mjs E:/snjallheimilid/tools/lines.json E:/snjallheimilid/tools/frames.json`), and what does not fit is said in a line under the frame. A position is pressed to move it (arrows, or press where it goes) or take it out, and dragged onto another to swap. The frame is drawn by vorur.js from the lines view's own shapes, which products.py writes into the page as data (`#pbData`), so it takes the colours and the plate of the line from site.css like the lines view; holding a frame it stays under the header. The frame is kept in the address as `box=line:colour:part,part` beside the filters. Tapping is the phone's way; dragging needs a mouse. Not on the page yet: Shelly and the other systems' products, Mosaic's frames counted in units, frames one above the other, and handing the frame to the estimate.
- `tools/film/` — the Blender remake of the Einn dagur film (owner 2026-11-03, from his reference picture of a new plan), rendered to MP4, on the page as `video/einn-dagur.mp4` (copy of `einn-dagur-blender-lett.mp4` from `E:\motion-tools\out`). `tools/film/house.py` builds the house, `tools/film/film.py` keys the six scenes and renders the frames, `tools/film/hud.py` draws the clock, captions, energy meter and end card and writes the video, `tools/film/timeline.json` holds the texts, clock and energy figures, `tools/film/fetch_assets.py` downloads the CC0 Poly Haven models and textures. `tools/film/energy.json` and the figures in the timeline come from the owner's own house, with his leave; the day in the film is put together from two real days. Assets, frames and videos stay outside git in `E:\motion-tools`. Run with `blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/film/film.py -- --frames DIR`, then `python tools/film/hud.py DIR out.mp4`.
- `site.js` — `AETLUN` = the estimate page's address (aetlun.snjallheimilid.is), set on every `[data-aetlun]` link;
  `FORM` = the Formspree id shared with quick.html, subject
  "Fyrirspurn af snjallheimilid.is"; the form posts JSON and shows the result in `#formMsg`.
  Also the `#dagur` film player (see index.html above). `LANG` is the page's language: index.html is Icelandic; the
  copies `en/index.html`, `es/index.html` and `pl/index.html` set `window.I18N` (lang and the few strings site.js shows), and site.js
  then takes the film and captions of that language and sends the estimate link with `?lang=`.
- Languages (owner 2026-11-04: English, Spanish, Polish): `tools/i18n.py` writes the three copies of index.html from the Icelandic one,
  every text node and text attribute (not svg, script, style) looked up in `i18n/en.json`, `i18n/es.json`, `i18n/pl.json`
  (Icelandic text -> translation; the hero svg's room names and circuit labels, the list in the script, are looked up in the same files); a text without a translation stops the build, `--dump` lists what index.html needs.
  Rerun it after any change to index.html. The header's `.lang` switch and the hreflang links are in index.html by hand.
  The film per language: `tools/film/timeline.en.json` (.es, .pl) carry the scene lines and the HUD's words (`labels`),
  `python tools/film/hud.py FRAMES out.mp4 --lang en` draws them over the same frames (E:\motion-tools\film\langs.sh does the
  three) into `video/einn-dagur.en.mp4` etc.; the 3D frames themselves stay as rendered, so the wall display says
  Allt slökkt in every language. The estimate page has its own tables (quick-i18n.js in smart-home-estimator).

Colours (owner 2026-11-04): the warm palette stays, and `--sky` / `--glass` in `site.css` are the logo's light colours, for lines and fills, not for text on the light ground.

## Rules
- Text is the owner's voice: draft it, he approves. Never invent prices, certifications or claims about him.
  The electrical work on a house is done by a licensed electrician; the site says so and must keep saying so.
- Icelandic first; English page later, same structure.
- Before ending a session: `python check_docs.py` (paths and ids named here must exist), update this file, commit, push.
